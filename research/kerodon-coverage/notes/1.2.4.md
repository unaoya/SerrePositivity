## 定義 1.6 （面 \(\partial_j\Delta^n\)）

\(0\le j\le n\) について、\(\Delta^n\) の部分単体的集合 \(\partial_j\Delta^n\) を

<!-- source: exposition/ja/infinity/t-01-definitions.html:76; block: t01-def-face-2 -->
<a id="t01-def-face-2"></a>

\[\partial_j\Delta^n([m])=\{\theta\colon[m]\to[n]\mid j\notin\theta([m])\}\]

<!-- source: exposition/ja/infinity/t-01-definitions.html:77; block: t01-def-face-3 -->
<a id="t01-def-face-3"></a>
